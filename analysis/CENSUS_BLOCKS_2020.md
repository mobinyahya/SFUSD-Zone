# San Francisco 2020 Census Blocks

`tl_2020_06075_tabblock20.zip` is the official U.S. Census Bureau 2020 Census
tabulation-block shapefile for San Francisco County (state FIPS `06`, county
FIPS `075`). It was downloaded from:

https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2020PL/LAYER/TABBLOCK/2020/tl_2020_06075_tabblock20.zip

SHA-256: `0f858e6c7748070b3f1a564ec39a55c2ef6913afb41dc6adb935621c771516b9`

The FRL analyses use each polygon's `GEOID20` to map student latitude and
longitude to the corresponding 2020 block.
